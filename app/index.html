📡 Relays

Your messages travel through these Nostr relays — always as ciphertext between unlinkable addresses. Everyone in a circle needs at least one relay in common. On the hosted (https) page only wss:// relays work; ws:// is for localhost testing.

⚡ Enable real-time editing?

By default, your edits stay on this device until you click Commit. Real-time mode instead sends each change to collaborators continuously as you type.

Your text stays end-to-end encrypted — relays never read it. But real-time mode does reveal to the relays when and how often you are typing. Manual Commit hides that by batching your edits. Only turn this on if you accept the metadata trade-off.
